memengine

A subscription service for people who want to quit social media but still want funny, positive, intentionally delivered memes. No doomscrolling. No negative algorithm. Just a small, controlled hit of humor in a branded mini page, email, or text.

Subscribe View meme page Share link

What people get

5-day or 7-day packs with a chosen number of memes and a chosen genre mix.

How it ships

Email and text deliver a link or embed to the branded meme page.

Why it exists

Funny + positive beats the controlled negativity of algorithmic feeds.

Product shape

1. Curate

We pick from a small set of meme genres and score each candidate for humor, fit, and safety.

2. Package

Each meme becomes a branded mini page with the content framed at the top and the subscription CTA at the bottom.

3. Deliver

Email and text send the link. The share flow keeps the brand attached when users forward it to friends.

Starter genres

We start with broad humor lanes, then narrow based on what people actually keep.

Relatable adult life Work / office Pet humor Dating / relationships Internet absurdism Pop culture parody Tech / nerd humor Self-deprecating Wholesome lift Dark-safe

Delivery options

  1. Email: best for a richer layout, bundles, and mixed meme packs.
  2. Text: link-first, short, immediate, personal.
  3. Private video later: YouTube Shorts or a similar mirror channel can come after the core flow works.

Build process

1
Lock the MVP.
One canonical meme object, one branded share page, email + text as the first delivery pipes.
2
Define the data model.
Memes, genres, subscribers, packages, delivery events, and referral tracking.
3
Build the mini page.
Fast, mobile-first, brand at the bottom, subscribe CTA always visible.
4
Add delivery.
Email and SMS send links to the branded page, not just raw media.
5
Learn from usage.
Track opens, shares, replies, and which genres people keep.
memengine is the brandable wrapper around a curated meme subscription system: less feed, more control, more humor, less sludge.
